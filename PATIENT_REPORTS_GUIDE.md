# Patient Reports & Medical History - Quick Start Guide

## 🎯 What's New?

Your DC Dental App now has three powerful new features to help manage your medical records:

### 1. 📤 Upload Medical Reports
Store important medical documents with your account

### 2. 📋 View Your Reports  
Access all your uploaded medical records anytime

### 3. 📜 Medical History
See your complete appointment history and patient profile

---

## 👤 FOR PATIENTS

### How to Upload a Report

1. **Open Your Dashboard**
   - Login to your patient account
   - You're on the Patient Dashboard

2. **Click "📤 Upload Report"**
   - A modal will pop up

3. **Select Report Type**
   - Medical Report
   - Lab Test
   - X-Ray
   - Prescription
   - Treatment Notes
   - Other

4. **Choose Your File**
   - Click "📎 Choose File" button
   - Select document from your phone

5. **Add Description**
   - Write notes about the report
   - Include any relevant details

6. **Upload**
   - Click "Upload Report" button
   - Wait for success message

### How to View Your Reports

1. **Click "📋 View Reports"** on dashboard
2. **Browse Your Reports**
   - See all uploaded reports
   - Most recent first
   - Each shows: Type, Filename, Description, Date

3. **Delete a Report**
   - Long-press or tap delete icon
   - Confirm deletion

### How to Check Your Medical History

1. **Click "📜 View Medical History"**
2. **See All Appointments**
   - List of all past appointments
   - Shows doctor, date, service, status

3. **View Details**
   - Tap any appointment
   - See full patient information
   - Includes date appointment was booked

---

## 👨‍⚕️ FOR DOCTORS

### How to View Patient Reports

1. **Go to Doctor Dashboard**
2. **Find Patient's Appointment**
   - Look through pending/confirmed appointments
   - Or use search to find patient

3. **Click "📋 Reports" Button**
   - Next to other action buttons
   - Purple button on appointment card

4. **View Patient's Reports**
   - See all reports they've shared
   - Read descriptions and details
   - Check upload dates

5. **Review Medical Records**
   - Use information for treatment planning
   - Reference previous medical history

## 📊 Report Types Explained

| Type | Usage |
|------|-------|
| **Medical Report** | General medical findings and observations |
| **Lab Test** | Blood work, urinalysis, other lab results |
| **X-Ray** | Radiographic images and results |
| **Prescription** | Medication prescriptions and dosages |
| **Treatment Notes** | Doctor's notes from previous visits |
| **Other** | Any other medical documents |

---

## 🔒 Privacy & Visibility

- **Patient Reports**: Only you and your doctor can see them
- **Doctor Access**: Only when you upload the report
- **Deletion**: Only you can delete your own reports
- **History**: Personal medical journey stored for future care

---

## 📱 Status Indicators

When viewing history or appointments, you'll see status badges:

- 🟡 **Pending** - Awaiting doctor confirmation
- 🟢 **Confirmed** - Doctor approved
- ⚫ **Completed** - Appointment finished
- 🔴 **Cancelled** - Appointment cancelled

## ⚡ Quick Tips

✅ **Do:**
- Upload reports regularly to maintain medical history
- Include detailed descriptions for clarity
- Check your medical history before new appointments
- Keep prescriptions and lab results organized

❌ **Don't:**
- Upload sensitive personal information not related to medical care
- Forget to add descriptions to reports
- Delete important medical records without backup

## 🆘 Troubleshooting

### Reports Not Uploading
- Check file is not too large
- Ensure you have stable internet connection
- Verify description is not empty

### Can't See Doctor's Reports
- Make sure reports are marked visible to doctor
- Ask doctor to check their appointment list
- Refresh the app

### History Not Showing
- Verify you're logged in with correct email
- Try refreshing/reopening modal
- Check if appointments were created
